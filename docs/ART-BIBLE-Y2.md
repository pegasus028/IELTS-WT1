## Style line (paste first in every prompt)

## Fixed lines (paste after the style line in every prompt)
- NO TEXT: No readable text anywhere in the picture — signs, books, papers, labels, maps, charts and clocks are blank or show unreadable squiggles. No captions.
- CROWD: Unless the scene text explicitly mentions other students or a crowd, include NO other people besides the named characters.
- SCARVES: Any scarf or tie uses only navy-and-silver, teal-and-copper, violet-purple-and-gold or crimson-and-cream. Never red-and-gold, maroon-and-gold, green-and-silver or yellow-and-black.
- UNIFORM: Students wear a long black open hooded robe with a small crest (a quill crossed over a lantern on a shield, midnight blue and brass), a white shirt, a midnight-blue-and-brass striped tie and a grey V-neck with brass trim. No Thai school uniform.
- ONCE: Each named character appears exactly once in the picture.
- LIKENESS: No resemblance to any film or TV actor; no round glasses with a forehead scar; no pointed wizard hats; no wands.

## Character blocks (paste the block of every named character in the prompt, word for word)

**The heroine (second year, about 16):** A teenage girl drawn in a clean modern anime style, light warm fair skin, a gentle oval face,
dark brown eyes, no glasses, no scar and no mole. One long glossy black braid, now reaching her waist, worn over one shoulder and tied with
a midnight-blue ribbon bow; soft side-swept bangs. A tan leather satchel with brass buckles, a small brass key hanging from its strap. A
navy-and-silver striped scarf. A small round brass second-year pin on the left lapel of her black robe. Slightly taller and more
self-assured than a first-year.

**Juno Marlowe (Bridge, second year):** A Black British teenage girl with warm brown skin, dark curly hair in two round puffs tied with
copper ribbons, an expressive, warm face. A teal-and-copper striped scarf. Often holds a notebook and pencil.

**Felix Okafor (editor of The Lantern, second year):** A Nigerian-British teenage boy with dark brown skin and short, neatly cut black hair,
a friendly grin. A brass box camera on a leather strap across his chest, and a small brass press badge on a cord round his neck.

**Celeste Ashby (Lexicon, second year):** A teenage girl with a sleek black bob grown to her chin, held back with a silver clip on one side,
composed and clever. A violet-purple-and-gold striped scarf — never maroon, burgundy or red. Carries her own worn brown leather spellbook.

**Ploy Saengkaew (Compass, first year, 12):** A small Thai girl of about twelve with light-to-medium warm skin, two short pigtails tied with
copper beads, a round young face, often pale and tired. A navy-and-silver striped scarf. Hugs a brown leather almanac to her chest.

**Professor Bartholomew Quist (Master of Reports):** A man in his forties with a tanned face, wavy chestnut hair swept back, a neatly
trimmed chestnut beard and a wide showman's smile. A mustard-yellow waistcoat under a long brown coat, a monocle on a gold chain. Often
with a stack of his own books whose covers show only his painted portrait (no readable title). Comic and vain, never menacing.

**Dr Halima Strand (Keeper of the Observatory):** A woman in her forties with warm olive-brown skin and dark hair pinned up with a pencil
through the bun; sleeves rolled to the elbow; ink stains on her fingers; a long brass ruler at her belt; a plain dark-green work robe;
no glasses. Calm, practical, observant.

**Professor Marisol Penhallow (Head of Examinations):** A tall woman in her fifties with a silver bob, a charcoal-grey robe, and brass-rimmed
spectacles on a chain round her neck. Dignified and precise.

**Dr Osric Hale (headmaster):** A clean-shaven older man with short grey hair, a tweed waistcoat and a dark jacket. Never a long beard.

**Mr Alder Fenwick (librarian):** An older man, lean, with a lined kind face and grey stubble, a long dark coat, a ring of heavy iron keys at
his belt.

**Marmalade (Fenwick's cat):** A grey-brown tabby cat with darker stripes — not orange, not ginger, not a fantastical creature.

**Nutmeg (the owl):** A grey-brown owl with soft barred feathers, now slightly older with paler feathers round the eyes. Never white,
never a pale owl with dark spots.

**Mina Sato (Compass):** A Japanese teenage girl with a high black ponytail and a navy-and-silver striped scarf.

**Heads of house:** Professor Rhea North (navy robe, short dark hair, compass-rose pin); Professor Tomás Brigg (teal robe, full dark beard);
Professor Wen Liang (plum robe, neat black hair, calm); Professor Ada Weaver (crimson robe, long white braid).

**Orrin Vane (memory only):** A tall, thin teenage boy from 1976 with untidy sandy-brown hair, freckles, a long narrow face and a cold, polished smile, in an old-fashioned black
school robe. In the present he appears only as a misty grey shape made of handwriting.

**Objects:** *The Blank Almanac* — a small brown leather almanac with blank cream pages; grey handwriting rises from it. *The Reflector* —
a huge curved brass mirror on a jointed clockwork arm with visible gears, breathing grey frost; not alive, no face, not a snake. *The
Gossip Quill* — a violet-black quill writing by itself. *The Tide Clock* — a tall glass case on the Observatory stairs with a round brass
face (no numerals) and five lines of coloured water inside, like a line graph. *The Frost* — pale grey frost that turns people and paper
grey and still; figures on paper go blank. *The Sorting Lantern* — a tall brass lantern glowing in four horizontal bands: navy, teal,
plum, crimson. *Living charts* — line graphs, bars, pies and maps made of soft light, with no numbers.
